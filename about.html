01 / THE STORY

A different field.
A familiar purpose.

My career is changing, but the part I value most stays the same: helping someone move forward.

As a nursing assistant at Bremer Heimstiftung in Germany, I learned to listen carefully, stay organized, and communicate with people who needed support. Accurate documentation, confidentiality, and attention to detail were part of my everyday work.

Now, I’m bringing those habits into Information Technology. Through my internship and hands-on training at i.c.stars, I’m building skills in Python, HTML, and CSS, working through practical assignments, and learning alongside a team.

IT support interests me because a technical issue is also a human experience. I want to understand the problem, work through it carefully, and explain the next step clearly.
